# Implement Go 1.27.4 as the Floor

Associated MADR: [0077-MADR-go-1-27-4-for-go-2026-6859.md](0077-MADR-go-1-27-4-for-go-2026-6859.md)

## Goal

The MADR's D1 to D3, as the owner approved them on 2026-11-11:

* `go.mod` requires Go 1.27.4, CI builds with it, and govulncheck passes
  with no exclusion;
* every current statement of the floor says 1.27.4, and R54 names
  GO-2026-6859;
* the change is on `main` before any code of
  [0076-PLAN-auth-error-kinds-and-doc-drift.md](0076-PLAN-auth-error-kinds-and-doc-drift.md),
  and ships in that PLAN's release, `v1.30.0`, with no tag of its own.

The MADR is `accepted`. This PLAN is `proposed`: nothing below starts before
the owner approves it.

## Rules for every phase

* **Approval.** The owner approves this PLAN, whole or by phase. A phase
  starts after the owner has committed the last.
* **No new gate, assertion or test.** This PLAN adds none, so it has no
  plant of its own. Two checks are seen to fail first, on purpose: Phase 1
  step 1's `make vuln` under Go 1.27.3, and step 7's build under Go 1.27.3
  after the change. If a phase turns out to need a new assertion, that is a
  deviation: it is recorded here first, and the assertion is seen to fail on
  a planted break with `python3 -B tools/plant.py SPEC.json`, on a scratch
  clone, never in the tree.
* **The gate, before staging:**
  * `python3 -B tools/gate.py` (the change holds `go.mod`, so `--markdown`
    refuses it);
  * `python3 -B tools/claims.py` on this pair, and on the two amended
    records;
  * `make records-check`;
  * `python3 -B tools/recordlint.py --changed`;
  * `python3 -B tools/denyscan.py --changed`;
  * `python3 -B tools/linkcheck.py`.

  Each exit status is captured before any filter.
* **Stage, then the owner commits** with `git commit --no-edit`. The
  disclosure guard runs over the outgoing commits before the push. The push
  happens on the owner's word.
* **Stop conditions:**
  * a fact of the MADR no longer holds;
  * golangci-lint `v2.14.0` fails under Go 1.27.4;
  * govulncheck reports anything under Go 1.27.4;
  * `go mod edit` changes more than the one line, or `go.sum` changes;
  * an exported name, a golden or a test would change;
  * a CI job fails.

  On any of these, stop, record a dated deviation here (with an amendment
  in the MADR when a decision or a fact changes), and wait.

## Rollout and Rollback

* **Order across repositories.** This PLAN's Phase 1 is the first step of
  the auth series in this repository, before 0076's code and its `v1.30.0`.
  gobble-cli raises its own floor by its own record; magic-cli-remote
  already has (MADR F7). Nothing in either waits on this commit, since
  each builds with the toolchain its own `go.mod` names.
* **Rollout.** Two commits on `main`, staged by the agent and committed by
  the owner: Phase 0's records, then Phase 1. No tag. A consumer meets the
  floor when it takes `v1.30.0`.
* **If the owner wants the floor in a tag before 0076 ships,** that is a
  deviation recorded here first: a patch release, `v1.29.1`, cut as
  0055-PLAN's Phase 2 cut `v1.20.1`. 0076's release is then still
  `v1.30.0`.
* **Rollback before `v1.30.0`:** revert Phase 1's commit. CI's govulncheck
  step then fails again on the next run, until another fix lands.
* **Rollback after `v1.30.0`:** a published version cannot change; a revert
  ships as a new release, by a record.
